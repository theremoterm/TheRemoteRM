Frequently Asked Questions

Can the review be carried out remotely?

Yes, within an agreed document-review and remote-discussion scope. Where evidence needs on-site verification, that limitation will be identified.

Will this guarantee a CQC rating?

No. These are independent advisory reviews, not CQC inspections. Only CQC determines its assessment outcomes.

Do I need to send care records through this website?

No. Do not share confidential records or personal details in the enquiry form. Any necessary evidence transfer will be agreed separately using an appropriate secure process.

What happens after I enquire?

We discuss what you need, confirm suitability and agree the scope, costs and delivery timetable in writing before any work begins.

What will I receive?

The deliverables depend on the package: the targeted review includes a feedback meeting and summary findings; the comprehensive review includes an action-plan report and follow-up consultation.
