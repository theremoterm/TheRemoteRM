Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 4 October 2026

The Remote RM ("we", "us", or "our") is responsible for the personal data submitted through this website. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how it is handled and how to exercise your data protection rights.

1. Data We Collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Name
  • Care Service/Company Name
  • Email Address
  • Details regarding your enquiry

Please do not submit special category data or information identifying people who use your service, such as names, dates of birth, care records or health information, through the enquiry form.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use this information solely to respond to your professional enquiries and provide CQC consultancy services. We do not use your data for automated marketing or newsletters unless explicitly requested.

3. Third-Party Services

Our contact form is powered by Formspree. Information submitted through the form is sent to Formspree for processing under its Privacy Policy. Please review that policy for information about its handling of submitted data.

If a consultancy engagement requires review of confidential or identifiable service records, we will agree a suitable secure transfer method with you before any records are sent. Do not send those records through this website form or ordinary email.

4. Data Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is needed to respond, manage any resulting professional relationship, meet legal or regulatory obligations, and handle any relevant disputes. We periodically review retained information and securely delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed, subject to any legal retention requirements.

5. Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. To make a request or ask a question about this policy, reply to the email address used in our response to your enquiry so we can identify and handle your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.
